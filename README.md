I’m **Davi**, a systems builder and co-founder of **L2 Systems**. I build software, AI tools and business automation. My work ranges from helping AI agents carry projects between sessions to sharing data between browsers and exploring rocket designs through simulation.

## Selected work

### [01 / ATLAS](https://github.com/L2-ootm/L2-ATLAS-PROJECT)

**AI work that continues beyond one conversation.**

A workspace that keeps agents’ tasks, tools, project history and approvals together. Built for continuing work across sessions and reviewing what happened.

`Public research preview` · [Windows package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@systemsl2/atlas) · [Architecture](https://github.com/L2-ootm/L2-ATLAS-PROJECT/blob/main/docs/architecture/OVERVIEW.md)

### [02 / Nodex](https://github.com/L2-ootm/nodex)

**Exploring whether the next copy can come from another browser.**

A browser-to-browser cache proof of concept, with freshness checks and server fallback. I refined its architecture through discussions with Professor Paulo Coelho at the Federal University of Uberlândia.

`Proof of concept` · External validation remains in progress.

### [03 / L2 Rocket Platform](https://github.com/L2-ootm/l2-rocket-platform)

**Computational search for better rocket designs.**

Software that generates and evaluates designs for different flight objectives. A fast physics proxy explores candidates; selected designs are checked with OpenRocket.

`Experimental software` · Simulation and design research.

I tutored **30+ classmates** across core school subjects, creating PDF study guides and later a web study platform shaped by their questions.
